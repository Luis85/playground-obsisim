# Colonist Lifecycle - Child, Adult, Elder, Death

Colonists age through three stages — child, adult, elder — and only adults can work, so every child is a ten-year bet and every elder a cost the colony planned for. Death comes from old age or from starvation; this is the increment that makes the colony able to fail, reversing Increment 1's deliberate "nobody dies."
