# Economy Readability - Runway and Bottleneck Status

Two additions from the increment's second polish pass: a per-resource "Empties in" runway on the Dashboard (how many ticks until a resource runs dry at its current net flow) and a per-stage bottleneck status (⚠ starved) on the Economy chains, so a production drop has a visible cause instead of being read off raw numbers.
