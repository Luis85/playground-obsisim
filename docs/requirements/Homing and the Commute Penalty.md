# Homing and the Commute Penalty

Where a colonist sleeps is now part of the map game: they walk from home to work, and a bad commute costs up to half their output. Housing beside a distant forester beats housing at the camp by roughly 1.9x, so clustering everything on the camp band is a tradeoff rather than the obvious play.
