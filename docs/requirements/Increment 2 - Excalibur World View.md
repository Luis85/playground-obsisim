# Increment 2 - Excalibur World View

The colony rendered as a live 2D tile world (Excalibur) in a new World tab: buildings with state rings and batch progress, workers colored by efficiency with tool coverage shown as a ring, hover-for-details on anything, and a legend that explains every encoding. Read-only in its day — Increment 3 made the canvas interactive. Also shipped two economy-readability additions: per-resource "Empties in" runway on the Dashboard and per-stage bottleneck status (⚠ starved) on the Economy chains.
