# Interface and Play

The work that decides where the screens put you rather than what the simulation does under them. Every interface item shipped before Increment 11 hung off the mechanic it explained — the World tab under World and Spatial Play, the Population view under Population and Survival — which is exactly why the interface itself was never anyone's increment, even as the game grew to eight commands and five disconnected surfaces for reaching them. Increment 11 gave it its own epic: one world screen, a dock of panels reachable without a tab switch, and a Ledger that is honestly a fallback rather than a second tab pretending to be an equal.
