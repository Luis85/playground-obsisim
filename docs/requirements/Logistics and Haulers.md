# Logistics and Haulers

Goods stop teleporting from a building's recipe straight into the stockpile: production now banks into an output buffer that must physically travel to the camp store, carried by haulers — a staffed role players assign like any other worker. Distance stops being cosmetic here (Increment 3 made position sim truth; this epic is the first to spend it): a building beside the camp is a one-tick walk, the far corner is thirteen. Increment 5's balance harness exists largely to measure this epic's gradient precisely enough to tune it.
