# Meals Per Head Gate Births and Nomads

Food is counted in meals per head — roughly years of food per colonist — and it gates both births and arrivals. "Recruit" became "Welcome a nomad," and the button now tells the player which gate is shut (no bed / not enough food / cooldown) instead of always looking available.
