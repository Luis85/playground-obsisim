# Population and Survival

The colony stops being a headcount and becomes people: colonists are born, age through child → adult → elder, and can die — of old age or of starvation. This epic reverses Increment 1's deliberate "nobody dies" and gives the colony a way to fail, and a way to grow itself, given beds, food and time. Housing, commute, and meals-per-head turn the map and the economy epics' work into inputs for a demographic system, rather than adding a separate one alongside them.
