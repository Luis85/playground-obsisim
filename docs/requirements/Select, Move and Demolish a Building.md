# Select, Move and Demolish a Building

Any building on the canvas can be selected, then moved (its workers walk after it, its batch stays intact — free and instant in this increment; Increment 5 later priced moving as distance-scaled downtime) or demolished (a confirmation, a full cost refund, and its workers walk home).
