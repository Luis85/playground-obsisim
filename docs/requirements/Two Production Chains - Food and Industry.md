# Two Production Chains - Food and Industry

Seven buildings across two three-tier chains: Gatherer's Hut and Farm→Mill→Bakery turn berries and wheat into bread; Forester and Sawmill→Workshop turn wood into planks and tools. Each building has one recipe (`inputs → outputs over N worker-ticks per batch`), consumes inputs at batch start, and reports "waiting for input" or "unstaffed" when it stalls — the value-chain pillar made concrete: refining multiplies a resource's base value rather than hoarding raw goods.
