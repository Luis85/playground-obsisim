# Two-Way Haul and Storage Buildings

Increment 4 shipped output-side haulage only: buildings push finished goods to the camp, nobody delivers inputs in. Both the Increment 4 and Increment 6 specs name this as the deliberate boundary and the same named successor — storage buildings with their own capacity, input delivery (two-way haul), and, per Increment 4's out-of-scope list, roads and real pathfinding in place of straight-line distance. Not started.
